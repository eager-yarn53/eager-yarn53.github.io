App: Quench Inch

Drag the mark along the bar. The named station is the last one whose stored inch is at or behind the mark. Hold that station to move the name to the next station. The mark stays. The last station holds and does not wrap. Set thickness between 0.5 and 2 to scale the displayed inch. Stored colors stay put.
