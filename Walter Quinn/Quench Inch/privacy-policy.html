Privacy Policy

App: Quench Inch
Developer: Walter Quinn
Contact: walter.quinn57@sina.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Quench Inch is an offline iOS tool for a heat-treat bar. It stores stations, a mark, and thickness on the iPhone. Dragging the mark names the last station the mark has passed. Holding that station moves the name onward and leaves the mark where it is. Thickness changes the displayed inch and leaves the stored color. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Station names, inches, colors, the mark, thickness, and saved runs stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers. If notifications are already allowed, a local notification can sound when a hold moves the name. The app does not ask for that permission on its own.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
