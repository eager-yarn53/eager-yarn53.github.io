Store each indent as a position from 0 to 40 mm and a Vickers hardness from 50 to 500 HV. The band starts at the fusion line and includes every indent out to the fusion line plus the HAZ width. Width runs from 1 to 16 mm. The named indent is the hardest one inside the band. A harder indent outside stays saved and is not named.

Drag the fusion line or type a new width. Saved hardness does not change. A width of 0 is rejected and the previous width stays. The last indent and the last coupon cannot be removed.

Privacy. See the Privacy Policy linked from the App Store listing. No account is required.
