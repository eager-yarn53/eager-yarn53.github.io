App: Haz Peak

Effective: November 20, 2026

Haz Peak is an offline iOS tool for one weld coupon. You store indent position and Vickers hardness. The heat-affected zone starts at the fusion line and runs the HAZ width toward the base metal. The named indent is the hardest one inside that band. A harder indent outside the band stays on the coupon and is not named. Moving the line or the width does not change a saved hardness. The app does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Coupon names, indent positions, hardness values, the fusion line, and the HAZ width stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
