App: Shim Pack

Effective: November 21, 2026

Shim Pack is an offline iOS tool for leveling machine feet with stacked shims. Each foot stores a target gap and shim thicknesses. Shims thinner than 0.05 mm stay on the foot and stay out of the sum. The named foot is the one with the largest positive remaining gap. Dragging the target does not change saved thicknesses. The app does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Foot names, targets, and shim thicknesses stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
