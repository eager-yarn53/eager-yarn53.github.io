App: Whey Gate

Effective: November 20, 2026

Whey Gate is an offline iOS tool for one cheese hoop. You log whey volume and pH in the same minute. The press is released only when the drip is under the line and the pH is inside the band. A later reading can reopen that press. Saved volume and pH stay. The app does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Hoop names, minutes, volumes, pH readings, the drip line, and the pH band stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers. If notifications are already allowed, a local notification can name the new status when a later reading reopens the press. The app does not ask for that permission on its own.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
