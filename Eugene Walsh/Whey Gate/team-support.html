App: Whey Gate
Developer: Eugene Walsh
Contact: eugene.walsh46@yahoo.com

Log whey volume and pH for the same minute. Volume runs from 0 to 2000 mL. pH runs from 0 to 14. Drip is the drop from the previous volume, divided by the minutes between the two readings. The line runs from 1 to 80 mL per minute. The pH band starts between 4.6 and 6.0, and the high end stays above the low end and at or below 7.0.

Both gates must pass before the press is Done. A later reading that fails a gate reopens it. The earlier volume and pH stay. The last hoop cannot be removed.
