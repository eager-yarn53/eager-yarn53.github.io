App: Seam Rivet
Developer: Frances Mercer
Contact: frances.mercer90@163.com

Rivets are placed along one seam, from 0 to 24 inches. Drag a rivet to a new inch. The named gap is the first opening wider than the pitch. When every gap is within the pitch, the last gap is named and the status is Tight. Pitch runs from 0.75 to 3 inches and does not move the rivets.

Two rivets must remain. A rivet that would land within 0.05 inch of another one stays where it was. Saved readings can be compared on this iPhone.

Privacy. See the Privacy Policy linked from the App Store listing. No account is required.
