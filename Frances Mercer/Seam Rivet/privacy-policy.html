Privacy Policy

App: Seam Rivet
Developer: Frances Mercer
Contact: frances.mercer90@163.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Seam Rivet is an offline iOS tool for one lap seam. Rivets sit along the seam in inches. You drag a rivet, and the named gap is the first opening wider than the pitch. Changing the pitch changes that limit and does not move the rivets. The app does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Rivet names, positions, pitch, and saved readings stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers. If notifications are already allowed, a local notification can appear when the named pair changes. The app does not ask for that permission on its own.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
