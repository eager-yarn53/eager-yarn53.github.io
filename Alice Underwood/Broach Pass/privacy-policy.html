App: Broach Pass

Effective: November 20, 2026

Broach Pass is an offline iOS tool for one keyway broach. Teeth run from the shank to the tip, and each tooth keeps its own rise. You drag a rise, and the named tooth is the first one that will not fit in the stock that is left. A pass toward the tip cuts only the tooth in front of that named tooth. Changing the stock changes only what is left. The app does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Tooth names, rises, stock, and recorded passes stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers. If notifications are already allowed, a local notification can appear when a pass is taken. The app does not ask for that permission on its own.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
