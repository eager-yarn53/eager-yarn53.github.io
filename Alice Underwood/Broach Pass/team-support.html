Teeth are ordered from the shank to the tip. Drag a tooth to change its rise, from 0.001 to 0.030. Stock left is the stock minus the passes already taken, and it does not go below zero. The named tooth is the first rise that is greater than the stock left. When every rise fits, the last tooth is named and the status is Clear.

Take a pass toward the tip. The app cuts the tooth immediately in front of the named tooth and leaves the order unchanged. If the first tooth already exceeds the stock left, it holds. If nothing is left, the stock is gone. Changing the stock does not change saved rises. The last tooth cannot be deleted.
