Rotor Hole names one hole on a single balancing plane. Enter each hole’s angle and mass. A hole lighter than the minimum is kept and left out of the residual. Drag the angle to see which seated hole lines up with the residual. Saved grams do not change.

The last hole and the last rotor stay. There is no account and nothing to buy. Readings remain on this iPhone.
