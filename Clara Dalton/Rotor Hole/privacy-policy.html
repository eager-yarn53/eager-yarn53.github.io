Privacy Policy

App: Rotor Hole
Developer: Clara Dalton
Contact: clara.dalton85@gmail.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Rotor Hole is an offline iOS tool for one balancing plane. Each hole stores an angle and a mass. A hole lighter than the minimum stays on the rotor and stays out of the residual. The named hole is the seated hole whose mass vector lines up most with that residual. Dragging the angle does not change the saved grams. The app does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Rotor names, hole angles, masses, and the common radius stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
