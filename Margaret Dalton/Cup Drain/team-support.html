Cup Drain names a batch of efflux pours. Enter each pour in seconds. A pour shorter than the cup intercept is kept and left out of the mean. Drag the temperature slope to see whether the corrected viscosities still sit inside the specification and inside the repeat limit. Saved seconds do not change.

The last pour and the last batch stay. There is no account and nothing to buy. Readings remain on this iPhone.
