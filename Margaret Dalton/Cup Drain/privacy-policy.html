Cup Drain is an offline iOS tool for one efflux cup. You store pour times in seconds. Kinematic viscosity is the cup constant times those seconds minus the intercept. A pour shorter than the intercept stays on the batch and stays out of the mean. A temperature slope corrects the viscosity. The batch is named only when every valid pour is inside the specification and the spread is inside the repeat limit. Changing the slope does not change a saved time. The app does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Batch names, cup constants, specification limits, and efflux seconds stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
